Together, the approvals give the Backpack entities both derivatives-trading and clearing qualifications for a regulated blockchain-based execution and settlement infrastructure. TLD Clearing is authorized to clear margin futures and fully collateralized futures, futures options, and swaps, although the source does not specify a product launch date.

rss · PANews · Nov 16, 22:27

**「Backpack’s Two-Step CFTC Approval」** Backpack’s path to the dual status proceeded in two steps: the CFTC registered its affiliate TLD Clearing as a derivatives clearing organization \(DCO\) on November 13, then designated TLD Markets, operating as Backpack Exchange, as a designated contract market \(DCM\) on November 16. The approval does not establish a launch date, customer eligibility, or listed contract schedule, so traders and clearing counterparties should confirm those details before treating the service as operationally available.

### [Poppy v1.0 and ChatGPT Wallet Advance Agent Commerce](http://www.geekpark.net/news/374075) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Sierra and Meta announced Poppy v1.0, a protocol that lets personal agents establish the assistant’s identity, the represented user and its granted permissions when accessing business services. It builds on OAuth, HTTPS, OpenAPI and MCP, exposes capabilities through a /.well-known/poppy discovery file, and is prioritized by Meta’s Muse by default; Shopify reportedly generated these files for merchants in more than 175 countries, and OpenAI is among more than 100 design partners. Separately, OpenAI announced ChatGPT Wallet for its dot assistant, allowing an agent to search, compare products, prepare checkout and request payment while keeping the real card number from the agent and merchant. Each purchase requires the user to review the items, total and card and approve them with face or fingerprint recognition before participating card networks issue a one-time credential with a temporary card number, security code and expiration; the limited release covers U.S. users aged 18 or older with ChatGPT Pro and eligible U.S.-issued Amex, Visa, Mastercard or Chase cards, using conventional fiat infrastructure rather than cryptocurrency. Although the releases occurred on the same day and overlap through Visa and Mastercard, neither project has confirmed that Poppy-authenticated agents can complete ChatGPT Wallet purchases, so the evidence supports parallel advances in trusted access and payment rather than a confirmed OpenAI–Meta integration.

rss · 极客公园 · Nov 17, 06:24

**「Earlier Poppy draft」** Poppy was first announced on October 6 as an open standard through which personal AI agents could identify themselves, sign in, and complete tasks with businesses. Earlier draft 0.1 addressed user permissions and session continuity across websites, APIs, and company agents while leaving payments to extensions; v1.0 is now enabled for Meta Muse users and every Shopify merchant. This history clarifies why ChatGPT Wallet is a separate development rather than an established core component of Poppy, and the available information does not confirm that the two interoperate.

**「Practical Effect」** Poppy gives participating sites a standardized way to publish supported actions and authenticate agents within both user-granted and enterprise-defined permission boundaries, while Shopify’s automatic discovery-file rollout makes the mechanism available across its merchant footprint without manual setup. ChatGPT Wallet is not yet general-purpose autonomous spending: its availability is constrained by geography, subscription tier, card eligibility and mandatory biometric approval, leaving high-frequency checkout friction and liability for mistaken or prompt-injected purchases unresolved.
