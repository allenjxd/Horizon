# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a long-form piece rewards careful reading for someone who builds with AI tools, practices vibe coding, follows Web3, works in product, or grows an independent media practice. Score the article itself, not the author's brand.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional.** An original or unusually clear insight about AI-coding workflows, AI products, Web3 systems, product practice, or creator growth, supported by evidence or first-hand experience, and likely to change how the reader works.
- **7-8: High value.** Substantial, transferable lessons in those beats: a concrete workflow, a well-argued product or protocol analysis, or a creator/growth case with real detail.
- **5-6: Useful.** Competent explanation that is incremental, narrow, or most useful to people already in that exact niche.
- **3-4: Shallow.** Familiar material, release notes padded into an essay, unsupported claims, or little beyond documentation.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotional, misleading, off-topic, or too incomplete to support its conclusions. General programming-language tours and generic career posts belong here unless they clearly serve the target beats.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider the motivating problem, originality, depth, evidence, honesty about tradeoffs, and whether a builder or creator could apply the lesson this week. Do not reward length, marketing language, or reputation by themselves.

Use three to five specific topic tags such as `ai-tools`, `vibecoding`, `web3`, `product`, `creator`, or `tech`.
