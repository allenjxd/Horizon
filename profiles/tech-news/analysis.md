# Evaluation goal

Evaluate timely news for a reader who wants to follow AI tools, vibe coding and AI coding agents, Web3, product practice, the creator economy, and engineering-direction trends. Score an item by how much it changes what this reader should do, build, or pay attention to next.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** A major AI product or model change, a protocol or platform shift in Web3, a product-practice change with broad consequences, or an engineering-direction turn that will reshape how people build.
- **7-8: High value.** A concrete new AI tool or workflow, a meaningful Cursor / Claude Code / Codex / agent-coding change, a substantial Web3 development beyond price action, a useful product launch or teardown, or a creator-economy development with transferable lessons.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental but real updates in the target beats; worthwhile if time allows, including narrower launches and solid secondary reporting.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine point releases of general programming languages or developer tools, minor version bumps, generic outages, thin aggregation, or stories only loosely related to the target beats.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, purely promotional posts, unverified rumors, day-to-day token or stock price moves with no product or protocol substance, celebrity gossip, or off-topic material.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer original announcements, primary documentation, and reporting that explains what changed and why it matters. Reward practical impact on builders, creators, and product people. Do not give a high score just because a well-known language, company, or token is mentioned. A Rust, Python, or compiler release should stay at 3-5 unless it clearly changes AI-coding, product, or platform workflows. A Bitcoin or ether price headline should stay low unless it is tied to a protocol, regulation, or product change. Treat community discussion as supporting evidence, not as a substitute for substance.

Use three to five specific topic tags such as `ai-tools`, `vibecoding`, `web3`, `product`, `creator`, or `tech`.
