# Technology news profile

Use this profile for timely developments that help the reader follow these beats:

- AI tools and new AI products or apps
- Vibe coding and AI coding workflows, including Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, and other coding agents
- Web3, including crypto, DeFi, onchain activity, L2s, and major protocols
- Product management, product launches, and product-led growth
- The creator economy, indie creators, and content-growth tactics
- Broader technology and engineering-direction trends that change how products are built or how industries move

Typical items include product and model launches, meaningful pricing or capability changes, protocol or platform upgrades, notable launches on product platforms, and concise reporting tied to a current event in the beats above.

Do not use this profile for complete long-form essays or tutorials (use a blog profile), finance-first coverage whose only point is a price move, routine toolchain or programming-language point releases, generic enterprise IT outages, celebrity or executive gossip without a product or industry consequence, or promotional material without substance.
